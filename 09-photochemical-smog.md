# Photochemical Smog — Formation, NCR Concerns, and Preventive Measures

## 10 Points

1. **Definition** — Secondary air pollution formed when sunlight drives reactions between NOₓ and VOCs. Visible as brownish-yellow haze. Also called "Los Angeles-type smog."

2. **Formation Chemistry** — NO₂ + UV → NO + O; then O + O₂ → **O₃** (ozone). VOCs consume NO, so O₃ accumulates. Further reactions form **PAN**, formaldehyde, acrolein.

3. **Conditions Needed** — High NOₓ + VOCs, intense sunlight, warm temperature, calm winds or temperature inversions. Worst on sunny, calm, hot days (late morning–afternoon).

4. **Key Components** — Ozone (lung damage), PAN (eye irritation + plant toxic), formaldehyde (carcinogen), acrolein (throat irritant), PM₂.₅, NO₂ (brownish colour).

5. **Health Effects** — Eye irritation, coughing, chest tightness, aggravated asthma, heart attacks on high-smog days. Children and elderly most vulnerable.

6. **Vegetation Damage** — Ozone is most destructive. PAN causes silvering/bronzing of leaves. Smog costs Indian agriculture ~$1.3B/year in wheat & rice yield loss.

7. **Delhi-NCR Causes** — 12M+ vehicles, stubble burning (Oct–Nov, 15–20M tonnes/year), industrial emissions, construction dust, Diwali firecrackers.

8. **NCR Meteorology** — Winter temperature inversions trap pollutants. Calm winds, low mixing height. Indo-Gangetic Plain acts as a "bowl." AQI regularly exceeds 400 (Severe).

9. **History** — Term "smog" coined 1905 (London). London Great Smog (1952) killed ~12,000. LA smog explained by Haagen-Smit (1950). Delhi crisis intensified since 2000s.

10. **Prevention** — GRAP (graded response at different AQI levels), BS-VI norms, stubble management subsidies, odd-even scheme, metro/EV expansion, N95 masks, smog towers, anti-smog guns.
