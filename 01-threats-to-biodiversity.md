# What Are the Major Threats Behind the Loss of Biodiversity?

## 10 Points

1. **Habitat Destruction** — Forests, wetlands converted to farms/cities. Amazon loses ~10,000 km²/year to cattle ranching.

2. **Overexploitation** — Overfishing, poaching, illegal logging. African elephants reduced from millions to ~415,000 due to ivory trade.

3. **Climate Change** — Rising temperatures alter habitats. 50%+ coral cover of Great Barrier Reef lost to bleaching since 1995.

4. **Pollution** — Pesticides, fertiliser runoff, plastics poison ecosystems. Gulf of Mexico has a 15,000 km² dead zone from nitrogen runoff.

5. **Invasive Species** — Non-native species outcompete locals. Brown Tree Snake wiped out 10 of 12 native bird species in Guam.

6. **Population Growth** — 8 billion+ humans demanding more land, food, water. Urbanisation encroaches on hotspots like Western Ghats.

7. **Deforestation** — 10 million hectares lost/year. Palm oil plantations replaced 50% of Borneo's forests, endangering orangutans.

8. **Illegal Wildlife Trade** — Worth $7–23 billion/year. Pangolin is the world's most trafficked mammal; rhinos hunted for horn.

9. **Monoculture Agriculture** — Diverse ecosystems replaced by single crops. Only 4% of North American tallgrass prairies remain.

10. **Disease Outbreaks** — Chytrid fungus caused decline/extinction of 200+ frog species. White-nose Syndrome killed millions of bats.
