# Interactions Between Society and the Environment — Impacts and Sustainable Strategies

## 10 Points

1. **Resource Extraction** — Humans extract minerals, fossil fuels, timber, and water for basic needs. Mining displaces ecosystems; overfishing depletes marine stocks. Example: India extracts ~90% of blue water for irrigation.

2. **Agriculture & Land Use** — Converting forests/wetlands to farmland destroys habitats. Pesticides and fertilisers pollute soil/water. Slash-and-burn farming releases CO₂. Agriculture uses 70% of global freshwater and 38% of land.

3. **Urbanisation** — Cities expand into natural habitats, fragmenting wildlife corridors. Generates heat islands, waste, air/noise pollution. 56% of world population is urban (projected 68% by 2050).

4. **Industrialisation** — Factories release pollutants (SO₂, NOₓ, heavy metals, effluents). Waste dumped into rivers/landfills. Industrial disasters (Bhopal 1984) show catastrophic risks of unchecked industry.

5. **Energy Consumption** — Fossil fuel burning = 80% of energy = largest source of CO₂. Leads to global warming, acid rain, smog. Hydropower dams alter river ecosystems and displace communities.

6. **Waste Generation** — 2B tonnes solid waste/year. Plastics pollute oceans (8M tonnes/year). E-waste contaminates soil with heavy metals. Open burning releases dioxins and furans.

7. **Deforestation Impact** — Reduces carbon sinks, destroys biodiversity, disrupts water cycles, increases soil erosion. India lost 1.6M hectares of tree cover (2001–2020). Amazon deforestation alters regional rainfall.

8. **Water Pollution** — Sewage, industrial effluents, agricultural runoff pollute rivers and groundwater. >80% of global wastewater is discharged untreated. Causes waterborne diseases (cholera, dysentery).

9. **Sustainable Strategy: Reduce & Restore** — Renewable energy transition, circular economy, reforestation, organic farming, rainwater harvesting, wastewater treatment, green buildings, promoting public transport.

10. **Sustainable Strategy: Policy & Participation** — Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) before projects, polluter pays principle, community-based natural resource management, environmental education, SDGs framework for balanced development.
