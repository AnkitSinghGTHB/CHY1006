# Montreal Protocol — Short Note

## 10 Points

1. **What & When** — International treaty to phase out ozone-depleting substances (ODS). Adopted **16 Sept 1987** (Montreal, Canada). Widely called the **most successful environmental agreement ever**.

2. **Why Formed** — 1985: British Antarctic Survey discovered the ozone hole. Molina & Rowland (1974, Nobel 1995) proved CFCs destroy ozone. 1 chlorine atom destroys ~100,000 O₃ molecules. Urgent UV-B threat.

3. **Substances Targeted** — CFCs (fridges, ACs, aerosols), Halons (fire extinguishers), Carbon Tetrachloride (solvents), Methyl Bromide (fumigant), HCFCs (transitional substitutes).

4. **Universal Ratification** — First and only UN treaty ratified by ALL **198 member states**. Unprecedented global consensus due to clear science and visible threat.

5. **Phase-Out Timeline** — CFCs: developed by 1996, developing by 2010. Halons: 1994/2010. HCFCs: 2030/2040. Developing countries got 10-year grace periods + financial support via **Multilateral Fund** ($4B+ disbursed).

6. **Kigali Amendment (2016)** — HFCs (CFC replacements) are potent greenhouse gases (up to 14,800× CO₂). Kigali aims to cut HFC production by **85% by 2047**. Could avoid 0.5°C warming by 2100.

7. **Results** — 99% of ODS phased out. Ozone layer is recovering — Antarctic hole projected to return to 1980 levels by ~2066. Without the protocol, 2/3 of ozone would have been destroyed by 2065.

8. **Health Benefits** — Prevented ~2 million skin cancer cases/year by 2030. Will prevent 283M skin cancer cases and 1.6M deaths for Americans born 1890–2100. Also prevents cataracts and immune damage.

9. **Climate Bonus** — CFC phase-out avoided ~135B tonnes CO₂-equivalent emissions (1990–2010). Climate benefit is **5–6× greater than Kyoto Protocol's** first commitment period.

10. **Why It Succeeded** — Clear science + visible "ozone hole" threat + available alternatives (HFCs, later HFOs) + financial support for developing nations + industry cooperation (DuPont) + trade restrictions on non-parties + adaptive amendments. A model for future treaties. Celebrated annually on **16 September** (World Ozone Day).
