# Causes, Sources, Effects, and Mitigation of Air Pollution

## 10 Points

1. **Natural Sources** — Volcanic eruptions (SO₂, ash), forest fires (CO, PM₂.₅), dust storms, pollen. Mt. Pinatubo (1991) released 20M tonnes SO₂.

2. **Anthropogenic Sources** — Vehicles (~40% urban pollution), coal power plants, industries, construction dust, crop stubble burning, waste burning, biomass cooking.

3. **Respiratory Effects** — Causes asthma, COPD, bronchitis, reduced lung function. PM₂.₅ penetrates alveoli. Children & elderly most vulnerable. ~7 million deaths/year (WHO).

4. **Beyond Lungs** — Heart attacks, strokes (PM₂.₅), dementia & Alzheimer's (NO₂), low birth weight, fertility issues. Outdoor air pollution is a Group 1 **carcinogen** (IARC).

5. **Acid Rain** — SO₂ + NOₓ → sulphuric + nitric acid. Acidifies lakes, kills aquatic life, damages forests, corrodes marble monuments (Taj Mahal).

6. **Global Warming** — CO₂, CH₄, N₂O trap heat. Black carbon (soot) heats atmosphere, melts Himalayan glaciers. PM reduces visibility (Delhi smog < 200m).

7. **Agriculture & Economy** — Ozone cuts crop yields 3–16%, costing $11–18B/year. Healthcare costs ~$5 trillion/year globally. Reduces worker productivity.

8. **Tech Solutions** — Catalytic converters (CO, NOₓ), electrostatic precipitators (99% PM removal), scrubbers (SO₂), diesel particulate filters, improved cookstoves.

9. **Policy Measures** — BS-VI norms, NCAP (40% PM reduction by 2026), odd-even scheme, GRAP in Delhi-NCR, stubble burning ban + Happy Seeder subsidy.

10. **Individual Actions** — Use public transport/EVs, plant trees (1 tree absorbs ~22 kg CO₂/year), air purifiers indoors, monitor AQI (SAFAR app), avoid outdoor exercise on bad AQI days.
