# Global Warming — Causes, Greenhouse Gas Mechanisms, and Role of GHGs

## 10 Points

1. **Definition** — Long-term increase in Earth's average surface temperature due to enhanced greenhouse effect. Global temp has risen ~1.1°C above pre-industrial levels (and accelerating).

2. **Greenhouse Effect (Natural)** — Sun's radiation warms Earth's surface → Earth emits infrared (IR) radiation → greenhouse gases in atmosphere absorb and re-radiate IR → keeps Earth ~33°C warmer than it would be. Without it, Earth would be -18°C.

3. **Enhanced Greenhouse Effect (Human-Caused)** — Burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and agriculture increase GHG concentrations → more IR trapped → more warming. CO₂ rose from 280 ppm (pre-industrial) to 420+ ppm.

4. **CO₂ — Primary Driver** — Accounts for ~76% of human GHG emissions. Sources: fossil fuel combustion (coal, oil, gas), deforestation, cement production. Stays in atmosphere for 300–1,000 years.

5. **Methane (CH₄)** — ~16% of emissions. 80× more potent than CO₂ over 20 years. Sources: livestock (enteric fermentation), rice paddies, landfills, natural gas leaks, wetlands.

6. **Nitrous Oxide (N₂O)** — ~6% of emissions. ~300× more potent than CO₂. Sources: agricultural fertilisers (nitrification/denitrification), fossil fuel combustion, industrial processes. Also depletes ozone.

7. **Fluorinated Gases** — HFCs, PFCs, SF₆. Small quantities but extremely potent (up to 23,000× CO₂). Sources: refrigeration, air conditioning, electronics. Long atmospheric lifetimes (thousands of years).

8. **Water Vapour** — Most abundant GHG. Acts as a **feedback** — warming increases evaporation → more water vapour → more warming. Not directly emitted by humans but amplifies CO₂-driven warming.

9. **Evidence of Warming** — Arctic ice shrinking ~13%/decade, sea levels rising ~3.6 mm/year, ocean acidification (pH dropped 0.1), glacier retreat worldwide, more frequent extreme weather (heatwaves, cyclones, droughts).

10. **Mitigation** — Paris Agreement (limit to 1.5°C), transition to renewables, energy efficiency, reforestation, carbon capture, methane reduction from agriculture/landfills, phasing out HFCs (Kigali Amendment), carbon pricing.
