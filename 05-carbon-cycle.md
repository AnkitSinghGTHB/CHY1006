# Carbon Cycle (with Diagram)

> *"Some carbon atoms in your skin may once have been part of a timber tree"* — justified below.

## Diagram

```
       ATMOSPHERE (CO₂ ~0.04%)
         │              ▲        ▲
  PHOTOSYNTHESIS    RESPIRATION  COMBUSTION
  CO₂+H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆  C₆H₁₂O₆→CO₂  (Fossil fuels)
         │              │        │
         ▼              │        │
      PRODUCERS (Plants/Trees)   │
         │                       │
      CONSUMERS (Herbivores→Carnivores)
         │
      DEAD MATTER → DECOMPOSITION → CO₂
         │
      (Millions of years) → FOSSIL FUELS
         │
      OCEAN SINK (absorbs 25% of CO₂)
      → Carbonates → Sedimentary rock
```

## 10 Points

1. **Photosynthesis** — Plants capture CO₂ + H₂O → glucose + O₂ using sunlight. This is how atmospheric carbon becomes part of a tree's wood.

2. **Respiration** — All organisms break glucose → CO₂ + H₂O + energy. Continuously returns carbon to atmosphere.

3. **Food Chain Transfer** — Herbivore eats tree → carbon enters animal body. Passes up food chain. **This is how tree carbon ends up in your skin proteins.**

4. **Decomposition** — Bacteria/fungi break dead matter → CO₂ released. Also forms humus in soil for future plant growth.

5. **Ocean Sink** — Oceans absorb ~25% of human CO₂. Store ~38,000 Gt carbon (50× more than atmosphere). Marine shells lock carbon as CaCO₃.

6. **Fossil Fuels** — Dead organisms buried for millions of years → coal, oil, gas. Carbon stored in geological reserves.

7. **Human Disruption** — Burning fossil fuels releases ~36B tonnes CO₂/year. CO₂ rose from 280 ppm (pre-industrial) to 420+ ppm (now) → global warming.

8. **Deforestation** — Forests store 450B tonnes of carbon. Cutting/burning releases CO₂ AND reduces future absorption. Accounts for ~10% of global emissions.

9. **Ocean Acidification** — Excess CO₂ lowers ocean pH → harder for corals/shellfish to build CaCO₃ shells → threatens marine food webs.

10. **Sustainability Link** — Paris Agreement targets 1.5°C limit. Solutions: reforestation, renewable energy, carbon capture, soil carbon management.
